About Me

I'm Juan Vanegas, a 16-year-old developer from Barranquilla, Colombia. I wrote my first lines of code at age 6 and never stopped — that's over a decade of learning, breaking things and rebuilding them.

About two years ago I made the jump from personal projects to real work: since then I've delivered commissioned software to institutions and businesses. My strong suit is backend, web applications and — above all — automated solutions built around AI agents.

I run nodo.dev, where I help businesses get rid of repetitive work, and I lead the Crack The Code research seedbed at Escuela Normal Superior del Distrito de Barranquilla. When I'm not building, I make content about programming and AI.
